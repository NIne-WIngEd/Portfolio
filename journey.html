Early building

From competition to something useful.

Robotics taught me to make ideas survive contact with hardware. A water-reservoir controller for a 64-family building showed me how a small system could make an everyday difference. Through Roboment, I also helped more than 150 participants with training and troubleshooting.

Research

Learning to ask a narrower question.

I published two first-author papers in the Journal of Emerging Investigators while in high school. That work helped me learn how to form a testable question, document a method and explain what the evidence supports.

Asia Kakehashi took me to Osaka, Japan. The YES program took me to Wisconsin in the United States. Living and studying in different places changed how I approach unfamiliar problems and people.
