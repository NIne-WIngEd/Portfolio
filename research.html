I’ve moved between chemistry, physics, microscopy and polymers. Research gives those curiosities a method: a question, a way to investigate it, and a careful account of what the result establishes.

In school, a partly moldy piece of bread left me wondering what the instructions hadn’t helped me see. I tried again, and the experiment ended before the question did.
